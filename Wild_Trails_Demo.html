Wild Trails.

TYPE Website
YEAR 2026

ABOUT

The Experience

Wild Trails is a concept safari website designed around the feeling of adventure rather than simply selling a trip. The page combines editorial-inspired typography, earthy colors, spacious layouts, and subtle interactions to create a warm, premium travel experience.

The site guides visitors through safari destinations, travel packages, FAQs, and the story behind the brand before leading them toward a custom quote. Responsive navigation, animated details, interactive FAQ elements, and a quote modal add functionality while keeping the visual identity consistent across desktop and mobile.

The goal was to create a website that feels adventurous, sophisticated, and distinctly connected to the landscape it represents.
