STATIC MEMORY: 1998

A low-poly survival horror game inspired by the eerie atmosphere of the PSX era. Explore forgotten places, uncover lost memories, and discover what should have stayed buried.

ABOUT

Pixel Depth

STATIC MEMORY is a PSX-inspired survival horror project built around the eerie atmosphere and visual limitations of late-90s console games. The experience combines low-poly environments, warped textures, dark lighting, and unsettling spaces to create a world that feels strangely familiar yet completely wrong.

The project focuses on exploration, environmental storytelling, puzzles, and atmosphere rather than constant action. Players are encouraged to explore forgotten rooms, examine mysterious objects, uncover fragments of the past, and slowly piece together what happened inside the abandoned world around them.

The goal was to create an experience that feels like discovering a forgotten horror game from the PSX era imperfect, mysterious, unsettling, and hiding something beneath its surface.
