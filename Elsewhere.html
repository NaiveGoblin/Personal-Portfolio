What is ELSEWHERE?

ELSEWHERE is a liminal horror archive documenting places that feel strangely familiar, yet impossible to remember. Empty swimming pools, silent corridors, abandoned resorts, and endless blue skies create a world that appears peaceful on the surface, but carries an unsettling feeling that something is missing.

The project explores the quiet horror of isolation and distorted nostalgia. Nothing here is explicitly dangerous, yet every location feels like a fragment of a memory that doesn't belong to you. Dark violet tones, faded colors, and VHS-inspired distortion transform ordinary summer spaces into something mysterious, nostalgic, and deeply unsettling.
